How to merge PDF files with OnyxPDF

  1. Drop two or more PDF files into the tool (or click to browse).
  2. Drag the files in the list — or use the ↑ ↓ buttons — to set the merge order.
  3. Click “Merge PDFs”.
  4. Download the combined PDF from the output list.

Why do it in your browser instead of uploading?

  • No upload: merging happens on your device, so contracts, IDs and statements never sit on a third-party server.
  • No 25-file cap and no file-size cap — iLovePDF limits free merges to 25 files; here the only limit is your device memory.
  • Pages are copied without re-compression, so text, images and links stay exactly as they were.
  • Works offline once the page has loaded — no account, no queue, no waiting room.

Good to know (honest limits)

  • Password-protected files must be unlocked first (use Unlock PDF) — an encrypted file cannot be merged until its password is removed.
  • Extremely large files (100 MB+, thousands of pages) are bounded by your browser’s memory.
