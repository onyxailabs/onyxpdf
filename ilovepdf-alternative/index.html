What you stop doing when you switch

Uploading. Waiting for a task counter to reset. Discovering at download time that the good version of a tool is Premium. Re-doing a job because a free batch cap cut it in half. None of those exist here, because the architecture that causes them — your file on someone else’s server — is gone.

What you give up — honestly

Server-grade Office conversion on nightmare layouts; PDF/A archival output; multi-party e-sign workflows with audit trails; AI summaries; and a 25-language interface. If your work depends on those, keep iLovePDF for those jobs. For the other 90% of everyday PDF work, this alternative is faster (no upload/download round trip), private by construction, and free without an asterisk.

Trying it costs nothing

There is no account to create and nothing to install (though you can install it as an app for offline use). Open any tool, do the job, and check your browser’s Network tab while you work — you will see your files going nowhere. That is the whole pitch, and you can audit it yourself.
